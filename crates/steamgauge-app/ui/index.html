            In other words, and other languages

            The counts above are of the words typed. The app can also find where reviewers said the same thing in other words or another language, closest first. It has to prepare each game once for that.

                Compare

                Each bar is the share of that game's reviews raising the subject, against the widest in the table. Its colours are the reviews that praise it, that do both, and that complain, in that order.
