I have played this game for three hundred hours across two save files and four patches and every single one of those hours was spent wondering whether the next update would finally fix the camera or the inventory or the way the map refuses to show anything useful when you zoom out and yet I keep coming back because the combat when it works is the best thing in the genre and the music carries every slow moment and the writing has a warmth that most games of this size forget about entirely so I recommend it with every reservation I just listed and a few more I have not thought of yet but will surely remember the moment I launch it again tonight after work when the servers are quiet and the lobbies fill with the same twelve people who never stopped playing either and who will tell you exactly the same thing if you ask them in the chat which you should because they are the reason the game still feels alive after all these years of patches and promises and seasons that came and went without much to show for them apart from a new hat and a slightly different shade of the same old map that everybody already knows by heart
